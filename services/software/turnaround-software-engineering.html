The Mandate

Some projects are on fire: behind schedule, riddled with bugs, feared by the team that built it, and too important to abandon. These situations need a different kind of engineer — someone who can walk into chaos, find the real problems under the panic, and bring it back.

I do turnaround engineering on exactly these codebases. Stabilize the fragile system, triage what's actually broken versus what's merely messy, and chart the path to finishing — for the high-risk, mission-critical software that cannot simply be scrapped and restarted.

Engagement Scope
  • Rapid assessment of a troubled or failing codebase
  • Stabilization of fragile, bug-prone systems
  • Triage of critical issues versus cosmetic debt
  • Root-cause analysis of recurring failures
  • Delivery-path replanning and scope triage
  • Targeted refactoring of the riskiest components
  • Team process and engineering-practice repair
  • Mission-critical reliability hardening
How I Work

I stabilize first and judge later — stop the bleeding, get the system reliable enough to breathe, then separate the genuinely broken from the merely ugly so effort goes where it counts. The aim is a clear, honest path to delivery, not a rewrite that restarts the same failure.

Every engagement runs under a signed NDA and, where testing is involved, a Safe Harbor agreement. Legal and ethical, start to finish.

What You Receive
  1. An honest assessment of what's wrong and what's salvageable
  2. A stabilized system and a realistic delivery plan
