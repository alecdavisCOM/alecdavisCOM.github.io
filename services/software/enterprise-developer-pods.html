The Mandate

Sometimes the constraint isn't strategy; it's hands. A deadline that can't move, a system that needs more senior engineers than you can hire in time, a crunch that would burn out the team you have. Capacity pods solve that — a dedicated, senior unit that plugs in and delivers.

This is not warm bodies billed by the hour. It is a cohesive pod that integrates with your process, holds your standards, and ships — brought in for escalation, surge, or sustained delivery, and structured to make your team stronger rather than dependent.

Engagement Scope
  • Dedicated senior-engineering capacity pods
  • Team escalation for deadlines and surges
  • Fast integration with your process and standards
  • Mission-critical delivery under pressure
  • Specialized skill injection where you lack depth
  • Knowledge transfer to your permanent team
  • Flexible scaling up and down by phase
  • Quality and standards enforcement throughout
How I Work

Pods are built to integrate, not to colonize — they adopt your standards, strengthen your practices, and transfer knowledge so you come out of the engagement more capable, not more dependent. Senior engineers, held to a high bar, delivering against the timeline that actually matters.

Every engagement runs under a signed NDA and, where testing is involved, a Safe Harbor agreement. Legal and ethical, start to finish.

Questions
How is this different from staff augmentation?+

Augmentation hands you individuals to manage. A pod is a cohesive, senior unit that integrates with your process and owns delivery, requiring far less of your management bandwidth.

Can you scale the team up or down?+

Yes. Capacity flexes by phase — surge for a crunch, sustain for a build, wind down cleanly — with knowledge transferred to your team throughout.
