The Mandate

The gap between an idea and a working system is where most products die — either in analysis paralysis or in a quick hack that can never grow up. I close that gap: a sound architecture and a working prototype, fast, without painting you into a corner you'll pay to escape later.

Speed and soundness are not opposites when the architecture is right. I build the prototype to prove the idea and the architecture to carry it forward, so the thing that validates the concept is the same thing that scales into the product.

Engagement Scope
  • Full-stack system architecture design
  • Rapid working-prototype and MVP development
  • Protocol and data-model design
  • Technology-stack selection and trade-off analysis
  • API and integration architecture
  • Scalability and evolution planning from day one
  • Technical-feasibility validation of the core idea
  • Architecture documentation and handoff to your team
How I Work

I architect for the product you intend to have, then build the fastest honest prototype on that foundation — real enough to prove the idea, sound enough to keep. Technology choices are made on trade-offs, not fashion, and everything is documented so your team can run with it.

Every engagement runs under a signed NDA and, where testing is involved, a Safe Harbor agreement. Legal and ethical, start to finish.

What You Receive
  1. A system architecture with documented technology choices
  2. A working prototype that validates the core concept
  3. An evolution plan from prototype to production
Questions
Will the prototype be throwaway?+

Only if that's the right call. I architect so the prototype can become the foundation of the product where that makes sense, rather than defaulting to code you'll discard.

How fast is rapid?+

Fast enough to validate the idea in weeks, not quarters — while still resting on an architecture that won't have to be torn out the moment it works.
