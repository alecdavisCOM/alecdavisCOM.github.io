The Mandate

Laws and regulations are made through a process with its own gates, timing, and gatekeepers. A great argument that ignores the process dies in committee; a modest one that respects it can become law. Influence here is procedural as much as substantive.

I map the full path — the committees, the members, the rulemaking windows, the coalition needed — and build the strategy to move a priority through it or stop one that threatens you. All of it conducted lawfully, transparently, and within lobbying and disclosure rules.

Engagement Scope
  • Legislative and regulatory landscape and path analysis
  • Bill and rulemaking strategy and timing
  • Committee, member, and stakeholder engagement planning
  • Coalition-building for legislative support
  • Testimony, comment, and position-paper development
  • Regulatory comment and rulemaking participation
  • Defensive strategy against adverse legislation or rules
  • Compliant engagement and disclosure management
How I Work

Everything is done by the book — lawful, disclosed, and compliant with lobbying regulation. The strategy respects how the process actually works, because that is the only way to move through it reliably, whether the goal is to advance a priority or to defend against one.

Every engagement runs under a signed NDA and, where testing is involved, a Safe Harbor agreement. Legal and ethical, start to finish.

What You Receive
  1. A legislative/regulatory path analysis and strategy
  2. A stakeholder-engagement and coalition plan
  3. Testimony, comments, and position materials as needed
Questions
Is lobbying legal?+

Yes. Advocating to government is a constitutionally protected activity, conducted within lobbying registration and disclosure rules. I operate fully within those requirements.

Can you defend against a bill, not just pass one?+

Yes. Defensive strategy — slowing, amending, or stopping adverse legislation or rules — is as much a part of the work as advancing a priority.

What does a Legislative Strategy engagement include?+

It is scoped to your objective, not a fixed checklist. A Legislative Strategy engagement can span 8 distinct workstreams — from legislative and regulatory landscape and path analysis to testimony, comment, and position-paper development — and we fix the exact boundaries in writing before any work begins.

What will I actually receive?+

Concrete, usable output: A legislative/regulatory path analysis and strategy, A stakeholder-engagement and coalition plan, and testimony, comments, and position materials as needed. Everything is documented to a standard your team, board, and counsel can act on — not a slide deck that gathers dust.

How much does Legislative Strategy cost?+

Project-based, matched to the real complexity of the work. Engagements run from five figures to nine, and you will know the exact number up front — no open-ended billing and no surprises.

How do we get started?+

Reach me on an encrypted channel, we scope the objective together, sign an NDA, and I begin. No drawn-out sales cycle and no account managers — you work directly with the person doing the work.

How do you keep a Legislative Strategy engagement confidential?+

Under a signed NDA, on encrypted channels, with total discretion. Your name stays yours and the findings stay with you. In this work, trust is the entire business.

Do I really need Legislative Strategy?+

Moving policy through the process that actually governs it — lawfully, and with the whole path mapped. If you are carrying that exposure, the answer is yes. If you are not sure, a short scoping call will tell you quickly — and that conversation costs nothing.

Is Legislative Strategy legal and compliant?+

Yes. The work is conducted lawfully and within the relevant election, disclosure, and lobbying rules. Compliance is a constraint on the method, not an afterthought.

How do you measure success?+

Success is defined before we start, in your terms, and proven with evidence at the end — A legislative/regulatory path analysis and strategy. You get results you can verify, not assurances.

Can you work with my existing team and vendors?+

What happens after the engagement ends?+

Can Legislative Strategy be done under a tight deadline?+

Yes — compressed timelines are normal in this work. Tell me the deadline and the objective, and I will tell you honestly what is achievable in the window before you commit.

What do you need from me to begin?+

A clear objective, the access the scope requires, and a signed NDA. I handle everything else and keep the demand on your team to a minimum.
