The Mandate

When you operate across borders, governments become a category of risk all their own — policy swings, instability, sanctions, expropriation, the stroke of a pen that changes the rules overnight. These risks are often invisible on a balance sheet right up until they dominate it.

I assess that exposure jurisdiction by jurisdiction and build the plans to manage it: early-warning indicators, structural hedges, and contingencies for the scenarios that would hurt most. The goal is to never be surprised by a government, and to have already decided what you'll do when one moves.

Engagement Scope
  • Country and jurisdiction political-risk assessment
  • Government-action and policy-change exposure analysis
  • Instability, conflict, and sanctions-risk evaluation
  • Expropriation and asset-security risk review
  • Early-warning indicator and monitoring design
  • Structural and contractual risk-mitigation strategy
  • Scenario planning and contingency development
  • Crisis-response planning for sovereign events
How I Work

Assessment is grounded in how power and policy actually behave in each jurisdiction, not headline risk ratings. Mitigation is concrete — structure, contracts, insurance, and contingency — and paired with monitoring so you see the shift early enough to act.

Every engagement runs under a signed NDA and, where testing is involved, a Safe Harbor agreement. Legal and ethical, start to finish.

What You Receive
  1. A jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction political-risk assessment
  2. A mitigation strategy across structure, contracts, and contingency
  3. An early-warning monitoring and scenario-response plan

Any organization with cross-border operations, assets, or dependencies exposed to government action, instability, or sanctions. The more jurisdictions you touch, the more it matters.

Yes. Beyond assessment, the work builds concrete mitigation — structural hedges, contractual protections, insurance, and contingency plans — plus monitoring to trigger them in time.

Concrete, usable output: A jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction political-risk assessment, A mitigation strategy across structure, contracts, and contingency, and an early-warning monitoring and scenario-response plan.
