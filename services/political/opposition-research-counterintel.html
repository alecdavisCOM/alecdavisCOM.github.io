The Mandate

Every campaign has two research files: theirs and yours. The first is the public record of the opponent — votes, statements, finances, contradictions — assembled into a picture sharper than anything they've admitted. The second is the harder one: an honest audit of your own exposure, built before the other side builds it for you.

Surprise is the enemy's best weapon, and the only defense is to already know. I do the digging on both sides from documented, lawful sources, so your campaign walks in knowing where every blow lands — incoming and outgoing.

Engagement Scope
  • Comprehensive opponent research from public and documented records
  • Voting, statement, and position contradiction analysis
  • Financial, donor, and association mapping
  • Self-research / vulnerability audit of your own candidate
  • Attack-anticipation and pre-buttal preparation
  • Rapid-response research support during the cycle
  • Source documentation and verification for every claim
  • Counter-intelligence awareness and information-security guidance
How I Work

Everything is sourced, documented, and lawful — research that survives a fact-check and a courtroom, never anything obtained improperly. The counter-intelligence half is equally rigorous: I treat your own file as the opponent would, so nothing lands as a surprise on debate night.

Every engagement runs under a signed NDA and, where testing is involved, a Safe Harbor agreement. Legal and ethical, start to finish.

What You Receive
  1. A documented opposition research book with sourced claims
  2. A candid self-vulnerability audit and pre-buttal plan
  3. A rapid-response research capability for the live campaign

Is opposition research ethical?+

Rigorous, documented research from lawful public sources is a legitimate and standard part of campaigns. I do not use illegally obtained material, and every claim is sourced and verifiable.

Why research our own candidate?+

Because the opponent will. A self-vulnerability audit lets you prepare for every attack in advance, so the campaign is never caught flat-footed by its own record.
