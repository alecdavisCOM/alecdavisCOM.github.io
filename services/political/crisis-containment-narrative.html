The Mandate

A crisis is a vacuum, and a vacuum fills itself. In the first hours after a damaging story, silence lets opponents and the press write the narrative — and once a frame hardens, no amount of later truth fully dislodges it. The window to shape it is small and closing.

I move inside that window: a clear factual position, a frame that is true and defensible, and disciplined message control across every channel at once. The goal is not to spin — spin collapses — but to make sure the accurate, survivable version of the story is the one that sets before the damaging one does.

Engagement Scope
  • Rapid crisis assessment and exposure triage
  • Factual position and narrative-frame development
  • First-hours response plan and sequencing
  • Message discipline across spokespeople and channels
  • Press, stakeholder, and ally coordination
  • Reframing and narrative-recovery strategy
  • Monitoring and real-time adjustment
  • Post-crisis reputation rebuild
How I Work

Speed married to accuracy. I help you say something true, fast, and consistent — because the frame that sets first tends to win, and a defensible early position beats a perfect late one. Everything is built to survive scrutiny, not to buy a day you'll pay for later.

Every engagement runs under a signed NDA and, where testing is involved, a Safe Harbor agreement. Legal and ethical, start to finish.
