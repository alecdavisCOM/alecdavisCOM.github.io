The Mandate

Geography still decides elections — districts, precincts, the blocks that swing. Geo-fenced digital targeting draws the campaign to the map: specific neighborhoods, events, and boundaries reached with a message built for that place, not a blanket buy sprayed across a media market.

The precinct that decides the race gets the message about the issue that moves it. The event your opponents are holding becomes an audience for your rebuttal. Spend concentrates where votes actually live, and nowhere else.

Engagement Scope
  • Geo-fence design around districts, precincts, and events
  • Location-precise audience building and targeting
  • Place-specific message and creative development
  • Event and venue targeting strategy
  • Cross-device and channel coordination
  • Turnout and persuasion campaign execution
  • Geographic performance measurement and optimization
  • Compliance with advertising and disclosure rules
How I Work

Targeting is built around real geographic and behavioral data and tuned continuously on performance. Messaging is matched to place so each community hears what's relevant to it, and all of it runs within political-advertising disclosure and platform rules.

Every engagement runs under a signed NDA and, where testing is involved, a Safe Harbor agreement. Legal and ethical, start to finish.

What You Receive
  1. A geo-targeting plan mapped to your priority districts and events
  2. Place-specific creative and message sets
  3. A performance dashboard with geographic optimization
