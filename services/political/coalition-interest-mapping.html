The Mandate

Every issue sits inside a web of interests — who benefits, who pays, who decides, and who quietly influences the deciders. Most campaigns react to the loudest voice in that web instead of understanding its structure. Structure is where the leverage hides.

I map the full board: the stakeholders, their real incentives, their relationships, and the pressure points that move them. With the whole network visible, strategy stops being guesswork — you build the coalition that actually carries the outcome and apply force where it counts.

Engagement Scope
  • Full stakeholder and power-network mapping on a given issue
  • Interest, incentive, and motivation analysis per actor
  • Relationship and influence-pathway charting
  • Coalition-building strategy and target identification
  • Pressure-point and leverage analysis
  • Opposition-coalition assessment and counter-strategy
  • Alliance sequencing and negotiation strategy
  • Ongoing network monitoring as positions shift
How I Work

The map is built from real relationships and real incentives, not org charts — because influence rarely follows title. The result is a strategy grounded in how power actually flows on your issue, showing you which alliances to build first and where a small push moves the most.

Every engagement runs under a signed NDA and, where testing is involved, a Safe Harbor agreement. Legal and ethical, start to finish.

What You Receive
  1. A complete power-and-interest map of your issue
  2. A coalition-building strategy with sequenced targets
  3. A leverage-and-pressure-point analysis
Questions
How is this different from a stakeholder list?+

A list names people. A map shows how they connect, what each actually wants, and where influence flows — which is where strategy and leverage come from.

Does this cover the opposition's coalition too?+

Yes. Understanding the opposing coalition's structure and incentives is essential to countering it, so both sides of the board are mapped.
