Psychographic Voter Micro-Targeting

Reaching voters by who they are, not just where they live — the right message to the right mind.

Hire for This Work → All Political Services
The Mandate

Demographics tell you what a voter is. Psychographics tell you who they are — what they fear, value, and respond to. Two neighbors with identical census profiles can be moved by opposite arguments, and a campaign that talks to the average talks past both.

I build models that segment the electorate by motivation, then match message to mind. The persuadable voter hears the argument that actually reaches them; the budget stops burning on people who were never going to move. Precision is the whole point.

Engagement Scope
  • Psychographic modeling layered on top of the voter file
  • Persuadable-universe identification and prioritization
  • Message-to-segment matching and testing
  • Values and motivation clustering of the electorate
  • Persuasion and turnout model development
  • Channel optimization per segment
  • Experimental design and lift measurement
  • Ongoing model refinement through the cycle
How I Work

Models are built and validated on real response data, with experiments that measure actual persuasion rather than assumed effect. Everything operates within applicable election and data-use law, and the focus stays on efficiency — moving the voters who can be moved and not wasting a dollar on those who can't.

Every engagement runs under a signed NDA and, where testing is involved, a Safe Harbor agreement. Legal and ethical, start to finish.

What You Receive
  1. A segmented voter model with a prioritized persuadable universe
  2. A message-to-segment matrix validated by testing
  3. A channel and spend plan optimized by segment
Questions
How is this different from standard targeting?+

Standard targeting sorts by demographics and vote history. Psychographic targeting adds the why — values and motivation — so the message, not just the recipient, is matched to each segment.

Is this compliant with data rules?+

Yes. Models are built on lawfully sourced data and operated within applicable election and privacy regulation. Compliance is a constraint on the method, not an afterthought.
