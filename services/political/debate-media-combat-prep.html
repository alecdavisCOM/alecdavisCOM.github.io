The Mandate

A debate or a hostile interview is a performance under pressure where a single bad ninety seconds can outweigh a year of good work. The candidates who look effortless aren't lucky — they're prepared to a degree their opponents underestimated.

I run that preparation hard: every likely attack rehearsed, every weak answer rebuilt, the hostile question anticipated before it's asked. The aim is a candidate who has already been through the worst version of the room, so the real one holds no surprises.

Engagement Scope
  • Full debate preparation and mock-debate programs
  • Attack anticipation and response development
  • Hostile-interview and press-conference readiness
  • Message discipline and bridging technique
  • Weak-point identification and answer reconstruction
  • Body language, delivery, and presence coaching
  • Rapid-response and real-time adjustment drilling
  • Post-performance analysis and refinement
How I Work

Preparation is adversarial by design — I put the candidate through the hardest version of the room, with the toughest questions and the sharpest attacks, so the live event feels easier than the rehearsal. Every weak answer is found and rebuilt before anyone hostile finds it.

Every engagement runs under a signed NDA and, where testing is involved, a Safe Harbor agreement. Legal and ethical, start to finish.

What You Receive
  1. A debate-prep program with mock sessions and attack scripts
  2. A rebuilt answer set for every known vulnerability
  3. A delivery and message-discipline coaching plan
Questions
How much prep time is needed?+

As much as the stakes justify. High-stakes debates reward intensive, adversarial rehearsal; even compressed timelines benefit from focused mock sessions on the likely attacks.

Do you cover hostile interviews too?+

Yes. The same adversarial method applies to hostile interviews and press conferences — anticipating the hardest questions and drilling disciplined, bridging responses.
