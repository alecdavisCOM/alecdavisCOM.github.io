The Mandate

Strong fundraising works on two ends at once. The grassroots base — many donors giving modestly — provides stability, legitimacy, and a pipeline. Major gifts — a few donors giving transformationally — provide the leverage that changes what's possible. Most organizations are good at one and weak at the other.

I build the engine for both and the bridge between them: the grassroots infrastructure that brings donors in and moves them up, and the major-gifts program that cultivates and closes the transformational asks. A campaign that scales because the whole pipeline is working.

Engagement Scope
  • Integrated major-gifts and grassroots campaign strategy
  • Donor-pipeline and moves-management design
  • Major-gift cultivation and solicitation strategy
  • Capital-campaign planning and sequencing
  • Grassroots acquisition and upgrade systems
  • Mid-level donor development and bridging
  • Fundraising-infrastructure and data design
  • Campaign goal-setting and feasibility analysis
How I Work

I treat fundraising as one connected pipeline, not two separate programs, so grassroots donors have a path upward and major-gift prospects are cultivated deliberately. The campaign is sequenced and goal-set against real feasibility, so ambition is backed by a plan that can actually deliver it.

Every engagement runs under a signed NDA and, where testing is involved, a Safe Harbor agreement. Legal and ethical, start to finish.
