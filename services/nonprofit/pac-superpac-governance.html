The Mandate

Political money lives under a microscope. PACs, Super PACs, and hybrids each have their own rules on contributions, coordination, and disclosure, and the penalties for getting them wrong range from fines to criminal exposure to a scandal that defeats the cause the committee was built to serve.

I structure and govern these committees so they operate aggressively within the rules — the right vehicle for the goal, the firewalls that keep coordination lines clean, and the compliance discipline that holds up when regulators and opponents come looking. Structured right, run clean.

Questions
What's the difference between a PAC and a Super PAC?+

Broadly: traditional PACs can contribute directly to candidates under strict limits; Super PACs can raise and spend unlimited sums on independent expenditures but can't coordinate. Hybrids do both through separated accounts. Choosing correctly is step one.

Do you provide legal opinions?+

I build the structure, governance, and compliance systems and coordinate with campaign-finance counsel, who provide binding legal judgment on the hardest calls.
