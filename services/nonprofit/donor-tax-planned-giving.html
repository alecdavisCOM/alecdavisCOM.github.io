The Mandate

Major donors want to give, and the tax code rewards giving generously — but only if the gift is structured well. A cash check is the least efficient way to make a large gift. Appreciated assets, charitable trusts, and planned vehicles can increase both the donor's benefit and the amount your mission receives.

I design those structures: planned-giving vehicles and tax-optimized pathways that make large gifts work harder for everyone. The donor gets the efficiency they'd expect from sophisticated advice; the mission gets more, and often sooner.

  • Bequest and legacy-giving architecture
  • Tax-optimized major-gift structuring

How I Work

I design the structures and coordinate with the donor's own tax and legal advisors, because planned giving works best when the nonprofit and the donor's advisors are aligned. The emphasis is on lawful, well-documented vehicles that genuinely serve both the donor's goals and the mission's.

Every engagement runs under a signed NDA and, where testing is involved, a Safe Harbor agreement. Legal and ethical, start to finish.
