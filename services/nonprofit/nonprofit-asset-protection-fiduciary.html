The Mandate

A nonprofit's assets are held in trust for its mission, and the board is personally responsible for protecting them. Yet many boards don't fully grasp their fiduciary duties until something goes wrong — a loss, a conflict, a regulator's letter — and by then the exposure is personal.

I build the protection on both sides: the controls and structures that safeguard the organization's assets, and the governance that lets board members actually meet their duties of care, loyalty, and obedience. The mission's resources stay secure, and the people stewarding them stay protected.

Engagement Scope
  • Asset-protection structure and controls design
  • Fiduciary-duty framework and board education
  • Financial controls and oversight systems
  • Conflict-of-interest policy and management
  • Risk-management and insurance (including D&O) review
  • Board governance and committee structure
  • Reserve and asset-stewardship policy
  • Audit and oversight-readiness support
How I Work

I translate fiduciary duty into concrete board practice — the policies, controls, and routines that let directors demonstrably meet their obligations rather than hope they're covered. Asset protection is built into financial controls and structure, and D&O and risk coverage are reviewed so the people serving are genuinely protected.

Every engagement runs under a signed NDA and, where testing is involved, a Safe Harbor agreement. Legal and ethical, start to finish.

Questions
Can board members be personally liable?+

They can, if they breach fiduciary duties or fail to exercise proper oversight. Sound governance, documented controls, and appropriate D&O coverage are what protect directors — which is a core aim of this work.

What does ‘asset protection’ mean for a nonprofit?+

Safeguarding the resources held in trust for the mission through financial controls, prudent structure, reserve policy, and risk management — so the assets serve the mission rather than being lost to mismanagement or avoidable risk.

What does a Programmatic Asset Protection engagement include?+

It is scoped to your objective, not a fixed checklist. A Programmatic Asset Protection engagement can span 8 distinct workstreams — from asset-protection structure and controls design to risk-management and insurance (including D&O) review — and we fix the exact boundaries in writing before any work begins.

What will I actually receive?+

Concrete, usable output: an asset-protection and financial-controls framework, A board fiduciary framework and education program, and conflict, risk, and D&O-coverage review. Everything is documented to a standard your team, board, and counsel can act on — not a slide deck that gathers dust.
