Getting charitable capital to where it's needed across borders — compliantly, and without it vanishing into friction.

The Mandate

Moving charitable money across borders is where good intentions meet hard regulation. Anti-terror finance rules, foreign-agent laws, tax requirements, and each country's own charity regime all apply at once. Done carelessly, funds get frozen, deductions get denied, or an organization gets into serious trouble.

I design compliant routing: the structures and procedures — equivalency determination, expenditure responsibility, intermediary vehicles — that get charitable capital to where it's needed abroad while satisfying every regulator on both ends. International giving that reaches the ground instead of the lawyers.

Engagement Scope
  • Cross-border grant-making structure and routing
  • Equivalency-determination and expenditure-responsibility processes
  • Foreign-charity and intermediary-vehicle structuring
  • Anti-terror-finance and sanctions-compliance screening
  • Foreign-agent and local-registration compliance
  • Tax-deductibility preservation across jurisdictions
  • Grantee due-diligence and monitoring frameworks
  • Multi-country program compliance design
How I Work

Compliance is engineered into the routing itself — due diligence, screening, and documentation built into the process rather than bolted on after a problem. I design the framework and coordinate with specialist counsel in each jurisdiction so funds move lawfully and deductibility survives the trip.

Every engagement runs under a signed NDA and, where testing is involved, a Safe Harbor agreement. Legal and ethical, start to finish.

What You Receive

  3. A jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction compliance map
Questions
Can a US charity fund work abroad?+

Yes, with proper structure. Processes like equivalency determination and expenditure responsibility, plus sanctions and anti-terror-finance screening, let charitable funds flow internationally while preserving compliance and deductibility.

What's the biggest risk in international giving?+

Compliance failure — frozen funds, denied deductions, or sanctions exposure. The framework builds due diligence and screening into the routing so those risks are managed from the start.
