Turnaround & Distressed Non-Profit Restructuring

For the organization whose mission still matters but whose finances don't work — a way back from the edge.

The Mandate

Nonprofits fail for reasons that have nothing to do with whether the mission matters: funding dries up, costs outrun revenue, a key funder walks, leadership falters. When a worthwhile organization hits that wall, the question is whether it can be stabilized and restructured before it closes for good.

I do that turnaround work. Stabilize the finances, restructure operations to a sustainable footing, rebuild funder and stakeholder confidence, and chart the path back to viability — for mission-driven organizations in distress that are worth saving but can't save themselves alone.

Engagement Scope
  • Rapid financial and organizational assessment
  • Cash-flow stabilization and liquidity management
  • Operational restructuring to a sustainable model
  • Funder and stakeholder confidence rebuilding
  • Program triage — what to protect, pause, or cut
  • Board and leadership crisis support
  • Merger, partnership, or wind-down evaluation
  • Return-to-viability and sustainability planning
How I Work

Cash and candor come first — stabilize liquidity, then make honest decisions about which programs the organization can actually sustain. I rebuild funder confidence with a credible plan and keep every option open, including partnership or merger, so the mission survives even if the current shape of the organization must change.

Every engagement runs under a signed NDA and, where testing is involved, a Safe Harbor agreement. Legal and ethical, start to finish.

What You Receive
  1. A stabilization plan and near-term cash management
  2. A sustainable restructured operating model
  3. A return-to-viability roadmap and funder-confidence plan
Questions
Can a failing nonprofit really be saved?+

Often, yes — if the mission has support and the problems are addressed in time. Stabilizing cash, restructuring to a sustainable model, and rebuilding funder confidence can bring a worthwhile organization back from the edge.

What if the organization can't continue alone?+

Then a merger, partnership, or managed wind-down may best serve the mission. The work keeps every option open and honestly evaluated, so the mission's work survives even if the organization's form changes.

What does a Turnaround engagement include?+

It is scoped to your objective, not a fixed checklist. A Turnaround engagement can span 8 distinct workstreams — from rapid financial and organizational assessment to program triage — what to protect, pause, or cut — and we fix the exact boundaries in writing before any work begins.

What will I actually receive?+

Concrete, usable output: A stabilization plan and near-term cash management, A sustainable restructured operating model, and A return-to-viability roadmap and funder-confidence plan. Everything is documented to a standard your team, board, and counsel can act on — not a slide deck that gathers dust.

How much does Turnaround cost?+

Project-based, matched to the real complexity of the work. Engagements run from five figures to nine, and you will know the exact number up front — no open-ended billing and no surprises.

How do we get started?+

Reach me on an encrypted channel, we scope the objective together, sign an NDA, and I begin. No drawn-out sales cycle and no account managers — you work directly with the person doing the work.

How do you keep a Turnaround engagement confidential?+

Under a signed NDA, on encrypted channels, with total discretion. Your name stays yours and the findings stay with you. In this work, trust is the entire business.

Do I really need Turnaround?+

For the organization whose mission still matters but whose finances don't work — a way back from the edge. If you are carrying that exposure, the answer is yes. If you are not sure, a short scoping call will tell you quickly — and that conversation costs nothing.

Is Turnaround legal and compliant?+

Yes. The structures and programs are built to satisfy the applicable regulators, documented for scrutiny, and coordinated with your counsel where a formal opinion is required.

How do you measure success?+

Success is defined before we start, in your terms, and proven with evidence at the end — A stabilization plan and near-term cash management. You get results you can verify, not assurances.

Can you work with my existing team and vendors?+

Yes. I integrate with your people and tooling, transfer knowledge as I go, and leave your team stronger rather than dependent on me.

What happens after the engagement ends?+

You get a clear roadmap, and where it applies I verify the fixes and stay available for follow-up. The point is a result that holds after I am gone.

Can Turnaround be done under a tight deadline?+

Yes — compressed timelines are normal in this work. Tell me the deadline and the objective, and I will tell you honestly what is achievable in the window before you commit.

What do you need from me to begin?+

A clear objective, the access the scope requires, and a signed NDA. I handle everything else and keep the demand on your team to a minimum.
