The Mandate

Donors increasingly ask a hard question: what did my money actually accomplish? ‘We helped a lot of people’ no longer satisfies a serious funder. But measuring real impact is genuinely difficult, and vanity metrics that count activity instead of outcomes fool no one for long.

I build honest impact measurement: metrics tied to a real theory of change, data systems that capture outcomes rather than just activity, and automated reporting that turns the data into the story funders and boards need. Credible numbers, produced without a month of manual spreadsheet work each quarter.

Engagement Scope
  • Theory-of-change and outcome-framework design
  • Meaningful impact-metric definition (outcomes, not vanity)
  • Data-collection system and instrumentation design
  • Automated reporting and dashboard development
  • Funder and board reporting alignment
  • Impact-evaluation methodology
  • Benchmarking and comparative analysis
  • Data integrity and credibility assurance
How I Work

Metrics are anchored to a real theory of change, so what gets measured actually reflects mission progress rather than busywork. Reporting is automated where possible to kill the quarterly spreadsheet grind, and built to be credible — honest numbers that hold up when a sophisticated funder looks closely.

Every engagement runs under a signed NDA and, where testing is involved, a Safe Harbor agreement. Legal and ethical, start to finish.

By defining a theory of change that links activities to outcomes, then instrumenting the measurable signals along that chain. The goal is honest, defensible indicators of real progress, not false precision.

Largely, yes. Once the data systems are in place, dashboards and funder reports can be generated automatically, replacing the manual quarterly scramble with credible, repeatable output.
