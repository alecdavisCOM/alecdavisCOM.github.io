The Mandate

Not every entity needs a license, and not every license is worth its weight. A regulated vehicle buys credibility, banking access, and the right to offer certain services — at the cost of capital, reporting, and oversight. An unregulated one trades that credibility for speed and simplicity. The art is knowing which role needs which.

I design the mix: which entity in the group is licensed and where, which stays lean and unregulated, and how they fit together into a structure that is coherent, compliant, and fit for what each part actually does.

Engagement Scope
  • Regulated-vs-unregulated entity-role analysis
  • Offshore licensing strategy and jurisdiction selection
  • License-type matching to intended activities
  • Capital, substance, and reporting-requirement planning
  • Banking and payment-rail alignment per entity
  • Group architecture combining regulated and unregulated vehicles
  • Licensing-application support and coordination
  • Ongoing regulated-entity compliance maintenance
How I Work

Every licensing decision is cost-benefit, not reflex. I weigh what a license actually buys against what it costs to carry, structure the group so each entity's regulatory status matches its real function, and coordinate licensed local professionals for the formal applications and opinions.

Every engagement runs under a signed NDA and, where testing is involved, a Safe Harbor agreement. Legal and ethical, start to finish.

What You Receive

  3. A compliance-and-substance plan for each regulated entity
Questions
Do I need a license at all?+

Sometimes no. Many activities don't require one, and an unnecessary license is pure cost and friction. The first step is honestly scoping which activities actually trigger a licensing requirement.

Is an unregulated entity legitimate?+

Yes, when its activities don't require regulation. ‘Unregulated’ simply means the activity falls outside a licensing regime, not outside the law. The structure still has to be compliant in every other respect.

What does a Offshore Licensing engagement include?+

It is scoped to your objective, not a fixed checklist. A Offshore Licensing engagement can span 8 distinct workstreams — from regulated-vs-unregulated entity-role analysis to banking and payment-rail alignment per entity — and we fix the exact boundaries in writing before any work begins.
