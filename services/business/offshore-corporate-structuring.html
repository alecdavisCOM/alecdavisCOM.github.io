The Mandate

A company's legal structure is its skeleton. Build it wrong and everything above it — tax, liability, financing, succession — carries strain it was never meant to. I design the skeleton deliberately: which entity, in which jurisdiction, holding what, answering to whom.

Offshore does not mean hidden; it means chosen. The right jurisdiction offers a specific, lawful advantage — treaty access, asset protection, regulatory fit, neutrality for cross-border partners. I match the structure to the actual objective and keep every layer defensible and compliant.

Engagement Scope
  • Jurisdiction selection matched to tax treaties, asset protection, and operating reality
  • Holding-company and multi-tier group architecture
  • International entity formation and registered-agent coordination
  • Beneficial-ownership and control-layer design within disclosure rules
  • Cross-border IP and asset-holding structures
  • Banking and payment-rail readiness for the chosen structure
  • Substance and economic-presence planning where required
  • Ongoing compliance, filing, and economic-substance calendar
How I Work

Everything is built to withstand scrutiny — structures that are legal, substantiated, and documented, coordinated with qualified local counsel and tax advisors in each jurisdiction. I architect; licensed professionals in-country execute and opine. That division keeps the structure both ambitious and clean.

Every engagement runs under a signed NDA and, where testing is involved, a Safe Harbor agreement. Legal and ethical, start to finish.

What You Receive
  1. A full structure diagram with entity roles, ownership, and control flows
  2. A jurisdiction rationale tying each layer to a defined, lawful objective
  3. A formation and ongoing-compliance roadmap with local-counsel coordination
Questions
Is offshore structuring legal?+

Yes, when done correctly. International structuring is a normal part of global business. The line is disclosure and substance: I build structures that comply with reporting rules and stand up to scrutiny, coordinated with qualified local counsel.

Do you provide the legal and tax opinions?+

I architect the structure and coordinate execution; binding legal and tax opinions come from licensed professionals in each jurisdiction, whom I work alongside throughout.
