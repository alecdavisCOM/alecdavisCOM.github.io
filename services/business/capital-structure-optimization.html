Enterprise Capital Structure Optimization

The right mix of debt, equity, and terms — so your balance sheet funds ambition instead of fighting it.

Hire for This Work → All Business Services
The Mandate

How a company is financed quietly governs what it can do. Too much equity and you give away upside you didn't need to. Too much debt and a soft quarter becomes a crisis. The optimal structure is specific to your cash flows, your volatility, and your ambitions — and it is rarely the default.

I engineer that structure: the mix of debt and equity, the instruments, the covenants, the timing. The goal is the lowest sustainable cost of capital that still leaves room to breathe when things go sideways, and keeps control where it belongs.

Engagement Scope
  • Current capital-structure and cost-of-capital analysis
  • Optimal debt/equity target and instrument-mix design
  • Covenant architecture and headroom engineering
  • Recapitalization and refinancing strategy
  • Dividend, buyback, and distribution policy
  • Growth, acquisition, and project financing structures
  • Rating-agency and lender positioning
  • Control and dilution modeling for founders and sponsors
How I Work

I model the structure against real downside, not just the base case — because the point of a good balance sheet is to survive the bad scenario, not to look efficient in the good one. Recommendations balance cost, flexibility, and control rather than chasing a single metric.

Every engagement runs under a signed NDA and, where testing is involved, a Safe Harbor agreement. Legal and ethical, start to finish.

What You Receive
  1. A cost-of-capital analysis and optimal target structure
  2. A recapitalization or financing plan with sequencing and terms
  3. A downside-stress model showing covenant and liquidity headroom
Questions
Isn't cheaper debt always better?+

Only until a bad quarter. Debt lowers cost of capital but reduces resilience. The work is finding the structure that is efficient and still survives your realistic downside, not the cheapest on paper.

Do you help with lender or investor conversations?+

Yes. Beyond the model, I help position the structure to lenders, rating agencies, and investors so the financing you want is the financing you can actually close.
