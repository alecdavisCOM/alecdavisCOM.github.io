The Mandate

Distress compresses everything. Decisions that deserved a month get an afternoon, and the wrong one ends the company. The first job is always the same: stop the bleeding. Secure the cash, buy time, and create room to think before the business runs out of road.

Then rebuild. Restructure the obligations, cut to a viable core, and re-earn the confidence of the people who can keep you alive — lenders, suppliers, staff. I have worked in the pressure that breaks ordinary plans, and I keep a clear head when it counts most.

Engagement Scope
  • Immediate 13-week cash-flow control and liquidity stabilization
  • Crisis triage and short-term survival planning
  • Debt and obligation restructuring and lender negotiation
  • Cost and footprint reduction to a viable core
  • Stakeholder management across lenders, suppliers, and staff
  • Operational turnaround and margin recovery
  • Contingency and insolvency-pathway planning with counsel
  • Rebuild plan and return-to-viability roadmap
How I Work

Cash first, always — a 13-week view goes in before anything else, because liquidity is the only currency in a crisis. From there I work in parallel: stabilize, negotiate, restructure, and rebuild, coordinating with insolvency counsel so every option stays open and legal.

Every engagement runs under a signed NDA and, where testing is involved, a Safe Harbor agreement. Legal and ethical, start to finish.

What You Receive
  1. A 13-week cash-flow model and liquidity stabilization plan
  2. A restructuring plan with lender and stakeholder strategy
  3. A return-to-viability roadmap for the restructured core
