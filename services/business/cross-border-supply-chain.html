Cross-Border Supply Chain Resiliency

A supply chain that bends under shock instead of snapping — engineered before the shock arrives.

Hire for This Work → All Business Services
The Mandate

Modern supply chains were optimized for cost in a calm world. That world is gone. One port, one supplier, one border, one sanction can halt a company that looked efficient on paper right up until the moment it couldn't ship.

I find the single points of failure hiding two and three tiers deep — the sub-supplier everyone depends on and no one contracts with — and engineer resilience in: diversification, buffers, nearshoring where it pays. Efficiency you keep; fragility you retire.

Engagement Scope
  • Multi-tier supplier mapping, including hidden sub-tier concentration
  • Single-point-of-failure and chokepoint identification
  • Geographic, political, and sanctions-exposure assessment
  • Supplier diversification and qualification strategy
  • Nearshoring, reshoring, and dual-sourcing analysis
  • Strategic inventory and buffer-stock optimization
  • Logistics-route and modal redundancy planning
  • Disruption scenario modeling and response playbooks
How I Work

I quantify fragility before prescribing cost — resilience is an investment, and it should be spent where the exposure is real, not sprinkled everywhere. The plan keeps the efficiency you've earned while removing the dependencies that could stop you cold.

Every engagement runs under a signed NDA and, where testing is involved, a Safe Harbor agreement. Legal and ethical, start to finish.

What You Receive
  1. A multi-tier supply-chain risk map with ranked chokepoints
  2. A diversification and sourcing strategy with cost/benefit
  3. Disruption scenario playbooks for your highest-impact risks
