The Mandate

The most expensive firewall in the world is one helpful employee away from irrelevant. Attackers know this; it is usually the cheapest way in. I test the human layer the way they exploit it — the convincing email, the confident phone call, the person who holds the door.

This is done with care, because the people being tested are your people. The goal is never to humiliate anyone; it is to measure real susceptibility and build a culture and a program that make the next pretext fail. Insiders, too — I help you see the risk from the badge that already works.

Engagement Scope
  • Targeted spear-phishing campaigns modeled on real adversary lures
  • Voice phishing (vishing) and pretext calling against help desks and staff
  • Physical social engineering and tailgating where authorized
  • Credential-harvest and payload-simulation landing pages
  • Executive and high-value-target (whaling) susceptibility testing
  • Insider-threat risk modeling and detection-signal design
  • Security-awareness program and metrics development
  • Help-desk and identity-verification process hardening
How I Work

Campaigns are authorized, scoped, and handled ethically — results are reported as rates and patterns, framed to improve the program, not to punish individuals. The output is a measurable baseline and a plan to move it, not a list of names to shame.

Every engagement runs under a signed NDA and, where testing is involved, a Safe Harbor agreement. Legal and ethical, start to finish.

What You Receive
  1. A susceptibility baseline across phishing, vishing, and physical vectors
  2. A prioritized human-layer remediation and awareness plan
  3. An insider-risk program design with practical detection signals
