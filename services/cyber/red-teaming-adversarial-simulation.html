The Mandate

A red team is not a vulnerability scan with better marketing. It is a goal: your crown-jewel data, a wire transfer, domain admin, the room with the server in it. I pick the objective with you, then I earn it the way a motivated adversary would — phishing, physical entry, network pivoting, and the quiet patience in between.

Most defenses are built against the attack the vendor sold, not the attacker who shows up. I close that gap by behaving like the attacker, leaving your team to hunt a live operator rather than review a checklist. What breaks in a contracted exercise is a line item. What breaks on a Tuesday is a headline.

Engagement Scope
  • Objective-based, full-scope engagements (digital, physical, human) scoped to a defined flag
  • Open-source intelligence and target development on the real attack surface
  • Spear-phishing and pretext campaigns built for your actual staff and vendors
  • External perimeter breach, then internal pivoting and privilege escalation
  • Active Directory / identity-plane compromise and domain dominance paths
  • Physical intrusion, badge cloning, and on-site network implants where authorized
  • Command-and-control tradecraft tuned to evade your specific EDR and SOC
  • Purple-team replay so your defenders learn each step with you in the room
How I Work

Every engagement opens with a signed Rules of Engagement and Safe Harbor letter, named emergency contacts, and hard de-confliction procedures. I operate quietly, log everything, and never risk production stability for a cheap win. You get continuous, encrypted status — not a surprise nine weeks later.

Every engagement runs under a signed NDA and, where testing is involved, a Safe Harbor agreement. Legal and ethical, start to finish.

What You Receive
  1. An executive narrative that tells the breach story in plain language, from first foothold to objective
  2. A technical kill-chain with reproducible steps, artifacts, and timestamps mapped to MITRE ATT&CK
  3. A prioritized remediation plan sequenced by real-world exploitability, not CVSS theater
Questions
How is this different from a penetration test?+

A penetration test enumerates vulnerabilities inside a defined box. A red team ignores the box and pursues an objective by any authorized means, testing your detection and response as much as your technology.

Will this disrupt production?+

No. Stability is a hard constraint written into the Rules of Engagement. I throttle, de-conflict, and stop on request. The goal is to prove risk, not to cause an outage.

What does a Red Teaming engagement include?+

It is scoped to your objective, not a fixed checklist. A Red Teaming engagement can span 8 distinct workstreams — from objective-based, full-scope engagements (digital, physical, human) scoped to a defined flag to active Directory / identity-plane compromise and domain dominance paths — and we fix the exact boundaries in writing before any work begins.

What will I actually receive?+

Concrete, usable output: an executive narrative that tells the breach story in plain language, from first foothold to objective, A technical kill-chain with reproducible steps, artifacts, and timestamps mapped to MITRE ATT&CK, and A prioritized remediation plan sequenced by real-world exploitability, not CVSS theater. Everything is documented to a standard your team, board, and counsel can act on — not a slide deck that gathers dust.

How much does Red Teaming cost?+

It is priced to scope, never a fixed menu. I take Red Teaming work from five-figure projects to nine-figure national mandates, and you get a clear, fixed scope and price in writing before anything starts.

How do we get started?+

Reach me on an encrypted channel, we scope the objective together, sign an NDA and a Safe Harbor agreement, and I begin. No drawn-out sales cycle and no account managers — you work directly with the person doing the work.

How do you keep a Red Teaming engagement confidential?+

Under a signed NDA and a Safe Harbor agreement, on encrypted channels, with total discretion. Your name stays yours and the findings stay with you. In this work, trust is the entire business.

Do I really need Red Teaming?+

A full-scope adversary turned loose on your estate, under contract, before a real one does it for free. If you are carrying that exposure, the answer is yes. If you are not sure, a short scoping call will tell you quickly — and that conversation costs nothing.

Can you work with my existing team and vendors?+

Yes. I integrate with your people and tooling, transfer knowledge as I go, and leave your team stronger rather than dependent on me.

What happens after the engagement ends?+

You get a clear roadmap, and where it applies I verify the fixes and stay available for follow-up. The point is a result that holds after I am gone.

Can Red Teaming be done under a tight deadline?+

Yes — compressed timelines are normal in this work. Tell me the deadline and the objective, and I will tell you honestly what is achievable in the window before you commit.

What do you need from me to begin?+

A clear objective, the access the scope requires, and a signed NDA. I handle everything else and keep the demand on your team to a minimum.

What kinds of organizations do you do Red Teaming for?+

Any where the stakes justify it — from founders to national-scale clients. The method adapts to your domain; the standard of discretion never changes.

How do I know I can trust you with this?+

Because discretion is the business, not a feature of it. NDA first, encrypted channels, and a record of keeping the quiet things quiet. Trust is earned one engagement at a time — I intend to earn yours.
