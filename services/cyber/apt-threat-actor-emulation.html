Advanced Threat Actor Emulation (APT)

The specific adversary that targets your sector, reproduced step for step against your defenses.

Hire for This Work → All Cyber-Security Services
The Mandate

Generic testing prepares you for a generic attacker. You don't have one. You have the specific crews that hit your industry — their tooling, their patience, their habits. I rebuild that behavior from current threat intelligence and run it against you in a controlled, intelligence-led exercise.

The point is calibration. When the emulation maps cleanly to a named actor's playbook, every gap it finds is a gap that actor would have used. Your detections get measured against the thing that is actually coming, not a lab curiosity.

Engagement Scope
  • Threat-intelligence-led actor selection matched to your sector and profile
  • Faithful reproduction of a chosen actor's tooling, infrastructure, and tradecraft
  • Full ATT&CK chain execution from initial access through actions-on-objective
  • Long-dwell, low-noise operations that test patience-based detection, not just loud attacks
  • Living-off-the-land and signed-binary abuse tuned to your environment
  • Detection-efficacy scoring per technique against your SIEM and EDR
  • Data-exfiltration simulation over realistic covert channels
  • Side-by-side purple-team tuning to close each detected and missed step
How I Work

Engagements are mapped to MITRE ATT&CK end to end so results are measurable, repeatable, and comparable over time. I coordinate with your blue team as much or as little as the exercise objective requires, and everything runs under signed authorization.

Every engagement runs under a signed NDA and, where testing is involved, a Safe Harbor agreement. Legal and ethical, start to finish.

What You Receive
  1. An ATT&CK heat map of detected, missed, and partially caught techniques
  2. An actor-emulation report tying each action to the referenced threat intelligence
  3. Concrete detection engineering recommendations, with example logic, per gap
Questions
How do you choose which actor to emulate?+

From threat intelligence relevant to your industry, geography, and data. We select one or more real actors whose targeting profile matches yours, then reproduce their documented behavior.

Is this a announced or unannounced exercise?+

Either. An unannounced run tests your SOC honestly; an announced, collaborative run maximizes detection-engineering learning. Many clients do one of each.

What does a Advanced Threat Actor Emulation engagement include?+

It is scoped to your objective, not a fixed checklist. A Advanced Threat Actor Emulation engagement can span 8 distinct workstreams — from threat-intelligence-led actor selection matched to your sector and profile to living-off-the-land and signed-binary abuse tuned to your environment — and we fix the exact boundaries in writing before any work begins.

What will I actually receive?+

Concrete, usable output: an ATT&CK heat map of detected, missed, and partially caught techniques, an actor-emulation report tying each action to the referenced threat intelligence, and concrete detection engineering recommendations, with example logic, per gap. Everything is documented to a standard your team, board, and counsel can act on — not a slide deck that gathers dust.

How much does Advanced Threat Actor Emulation cost?+

It is priced to scope, never a fixed menu. I take Advanced Threat Actor Emulation work from five-figure projects to nine-figure national mandates, and you get a clear, fixed scope and price in writing before anything starts.

How do we get started?+

It starts with one secure conversation. We define the objective, put an NDA and a Safe Harbor agreement in place, agree scope and price, and I get to work. You deal with me, start to finish.
