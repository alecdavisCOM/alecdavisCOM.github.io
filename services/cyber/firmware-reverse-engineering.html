The Mandate

Every connected device on your network is a black box running someone else's code. I open the box. Firmware extraction, disassembly, and analysis reveal the hardcoded credentials, the undocumented services, the broken update mechanism, and the backdoor the vendor swears isn't there.

This matters whether you build the device or merely trust it. Manufacturers get their product audited before an attacker does it in public. Buyers get to know what they are actually plugging into their estate before it earns a foothold.

Engagement Scope
  • Firmware extraction via flash dump, JTAG/SWD, UART, and update-package analysis
  • Bootloader, secure-boot, and chain-of-trust review
  • Static and dynamic analysis of extracted binaries and RTOS images
  • Hardcoded secret, key, and credential discovery
  • Update-mechanism and signature-verification auditing
  • Hardware interface and debug-port exposure assessment
  • Cryptographic implementation review on-device
  • Exploit development against discovered embedded flaws where authorized
How I Work

Work happens on a controlled bench with the physical device in hand. I document the toolchain and methodology so findings are reproducible, and I keep extracted firmware and secrets under the same strict NDA controls as any other sensitive client material.

Every engagement runs under a signed NDA and, where testing is involved, a Safe Harbor agreement. Legal and ethical, start to finish.

What You Receive
  1. A firmware analysis report cataloguing services, secrets, and attack surface
  2. A findings list ranked by exploitability and real-world reachability
  3. Hardening guidance covering secure boot, update integrity, and secret storage
Questions
We make the device — can you audit it pre-launch?+

Yes. Pre-release firmware audits catch the hardcoded key and the weak update path while they are still cheap to fix, instead of after they ship to a hundred thousand units.

Can you work from just the binary?+

Often, yes. With physical access I can extract firmware directly; with only an image or update package I can usually still unpack and analyze it.

What does a Firmware engagement include?+

It is scoped to your objective, not a fixed checklist. A Firmware engagement can span 8 distinct workstreams — from firmware extraction via flash dump, JTAG/SWD, UART, and update-package analysis to update-mechanism and signature-verification auditing — and we fix the exact boundaries in writing before any work begins.

What will I actually receive?+

Concrete, usable output: A firmware analysis report cataloguing services, secrets, and attack surface, A findings list ranked by exploitability and real-world reachability, and hardening guidance covering secure boot, update integrity, and secret storage. Everything is documented to a standard your team, board, and counsel can act on — not a slide deck that gathers dust.

How much does Firmware cost?+

How do we get started?+

Reach me on an encrypted channel, we scope the objective together, sign an NDA and a Safe Harbor agreement, and I begin. No drawn-out sales cycle and no account managers — you work directly with the person doing the work.
