About

Alec Davis

Reformed black-hat. Professional operator. Founder, investor, and lifelong researcher who happens to be very hard to surprise.

Origin

The FBI learned my name before I was old enough to drive.

I was expelled twice before high school — once in elementary, once in middle school — both times for the same thing: getting into systems I was told I couldn't. Somewhere in there came a formal, not-so-voluntary sit-down with federal agents about certain activity in the cyber world. I'll leave it there.

I was eleven when the obsession started and it has never once let go. I am twenty-nine now, and the only thing that changed is which side of the contract I sign.

The Mind

I research for a living and for a compulsion. Every day is a day to learn something — a protocol, a market, a jurisdiction, a human weakness. My mind wanders and it wonders, and I have stopped fighting it. That restlessness is the product.

I have been studying offensive security without a break since the sixth grade. That is not a credential on a wall; it is eighteen years of pattern recognition that lets me see the move before it's made.

The Turn

Black-hat taught me how things really break. White-hat is what I do with it. The skills are identical; the intent is the opposite. I use them to protect the people and institutions that hire me — and I only use them legally, under a Safe Harbor agreement and an NDA, every single time.

I am a founder, an investor, and a philanthropist at heart. All of it points at one thing I intend to finish.

What I Do Now

The work today.

Hacking, professionally. Bug bounties, retained red teams, blue-team hardening. Companies hire me to attack them before someone worse does, and to rebuild what I break stronger than it was.

Business consulting. Corporate and offshore structuring, licensing, M&A integration, turnaround. The architecture a company stands on.

Political consulting. Data, narrative, opposition intelligence, and mobilization for campaigns and causes that intend to win.

Software engineering. Polyglot, low-level, distributed. The hard builds most teams won't take.

Non-profit & philanthropy. Dual-track structures, endowments, planned giving. Missions built to outlast their founders.
